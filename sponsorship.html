Why become a DataConf Sponsor?

DataConf is a non-profit conference for practitioners, by practitioners.

Your support is a direct investment in the local tech community, helping us create a vital space for sharing real-world experience and fostering genuine connections.

🤝
Support a Grassroots Movement
Show your commitment to the data community. As a non-profit event, your support directly funds a high-quality, accessible conference that helps local tech professionals learn, connect, and grow.
💡
Engage in Authentic Conversations
Connect with an audience that values substance. Our delegates are hands-on practitioners who come for deep technical discussions, not sales pitches. It's a space where real-world challenges are shared and practical solutions are discovered.
🎯
Targeted Brand Visibility
Place your brand in front of a niche and highly relevant audience of data professionals. Your support will be acknowledged by a community that truly appreciates your contribution.
